How the rubrics work

  • Every criterion is scored on a 1–10 scale using the descriptors below. Half points are not used.
  • Each criterion has a weight. The assignment score is the weighted average of the criterion scores, multiplied by 10, giving a 0–100 score that is entered in the grade sheet. Example: criteria scores 8, 9, 7, 8, 6 with weights 25/20/25/15/15 → weighted average 7.75 → 77.5.
  • Format and submission requirements (student ID, name, and title on the first page; no title page; academic essay format; APA 7th; PDF; file name ID_NAME_ASSIGNMENT.pdf) are scored in the last criterion of each rubric. A file that cannot be opened, is not a PDF where a PDF is required, or has no identification receives a maximum of 5 on that criterion and is returned for resubmission.
  • Late work: minus 10 points per calendar day unless an extension was agreed in advance.
  • Weight of each assessment in the course grade: Three Research Ideas 2%, Literature Review 2%, CITI Training 2%, Method 2%, Final Term Paper 5%, Presentation 10%, Oral Exam 25%.

Common scale (applies to every criterion)

Score Level Meaning
9–10 Excellent Fully meets the requirement with precision, depth, and originality; nothing important is missing.
7–8 Good Meets the requirement; minor gaps or imprecision that do not weaken the work.
5–6 Adequate Requirement is addressed but superficially, unevenly, or with notable errors.
3–4 Weak Requirement is only partly addressed; major gaps, confusion, or errors.
1–2 Missing / Unacceptable Requirement is absent, off-task, or unusable.

2 Motivation and significance — why each idea matters (practical or theoretical problem) 20% Convincing, specific motivation for each idea; problem and stakeholders identified Motivation given for each idea, some generic Motivation stated but thin or only personal interest Motivation missing for some ideas No motivation

2. Literature Review Package (Literature.review.draft, 2%)

Task. Three documents: guiding table (Excel, 10–30 entries) 25%, annotated bibliography (PDF, 5–10 entries) 25%, literature review essay (PDF, 5–10 pages, APA) 50%. Each document carries student ID, name, and title.

2A. Guiding table (25% of the package)

# Criterion Weight 9–10 7–8 5–6 3–4 1–2
A1 Coverage and relevance — 10–30 peer-reviewed entries directly related to the research topic 35% 15+ relevant, recent, peer-reviewed entries 10–14 relevant entries, one or two marginal 10 entries but several off-topic or non-academic Fewer than 10 entries Table missing or unrelated
A2 Completeness of fields — citation, purpose/RQ, theory, method, sample, key findings, limitations/gap, relevance to own study 40% All fields filled accurately for every entry Fields filled; a few entries incomplete Key fields (findings, gap) often missing Mostly citations only Empty table
A3 Accuracy and usability — correct bibliographic data, consistent entries, Excel format, ID/name/title, file name 25% Accurate, consistent, ready to reuse Minor inconsistencies Several errors or inconsistent structure Many errors; not Excel Unusable

2B. Annotated bibliography (25% of the package)

# Criterion Weight 9–10 7–8 5–6 3–4 1–2
B1 Source selection — 5–10 high-quality, relevant peer-reviewed sources 25% All sources strong and central to the topic Sources relevant, one weak Mix of relevant and marginal sources Fewer than 5 or mostly non-academic No valid sources
B2 Summary accuracy — purpose, method, and findings of each source correctly captured 30% Precise, complete summaries in the student’s own words Accurate; some summaries thin Summaries partly inaccurate or copied from abstracts Summaries missing for some sources No summaries
B3 Critical evaluation and relevance — strengths, limitations, and how each source informs the student’s study 30% Insightful evaluation and explicit link to own research for every entry Evaluation present, links sometimes generic Mostly descriptive, little evaluation Evaluation absent for most entries None
B4 APA and format — APA entries and annotations, PDF, ID/name/title, file name 15% Correct throughout Minor APA slips Several APA errors Not APA Wrong format

2C. Literature review essay (50% of the package)

# Criterion Weight 9–10 7–8 5–6 3–4 1–2
C1 Organization and synthesis — themes or concepts organize the review; sources are compared and integrated, not listed one by one 25% Clear thematic structure; sources synthesized within and across themes Thematic structure; some sections are source-by-source Mostly a sequence of summaries Disorganized; no themes Not a review
C2 Coverage and depth — breadth of relevant literature, accurate use of 10+ sources, 5–10 pages 20% Broad, current, and deep coverage of the topic Good coverage; a few important works missing Narrow coverage or shallow treatment Very few sources; under 5 pages Insufficient
C3 Critical analysis and research gap — evaluates evidence, identifies contradictions and gaps, and derives the research question 25% Convincing critical argument; gap clearly established and linked to the RQ Gap identified; analysis partly descriptive Gap asserted without evidence No gap or RQ link None
C4 Conceptual framework and argument coherence — key concepts defined, relationships explained, framework consistent with the review 15% Explicit, well-justified framework consistent with the literature Framework present, some links unexplained Framework vague or disconnected Concepts undefined Absent
C5 Writing, APA, and format — academic prose, APA citations and reference list, ID/name/title, PDF, file name 15% Fluent and fully compliant Minor slips Bullet-heavy or several APA errors Hard to follow; incomplete references Non-compliant

Package score: 0.25 × (2A) + 0.25 × (2B) + 0.50 × (2C), each part = Σ(weight × score) × 10 → ____ / 100
